# DEEPBLOOM

An original cavern survival game about growing living light to protect the Bloomheart. Move with WASD or arrows, aim with the mouse, hold click to grow burning tendrils, tap to fire spores, press Space to dash and E to pulse. Purify seven depths and defeat the Rot King.
